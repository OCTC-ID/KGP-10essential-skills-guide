Teaching the Kentucky Graduate Profile

The Kentucky Graduate Profile names 10 Essential Skills that every graduate of a Kentucky public college or university should develop. This guide collects resources for teaching each one, organized by skill number.

See the 10 skills →

The 10 Essential Skills

  1. Essential Skill 1

    Communication

    Communicate effectively
  2. Essential Skill 2

    Critical and Creative Thinking

    Think critically to solve problems and create new ideas and solutions
  3. Essential Skill 3

    Quantitative Reasoning

    Apply quantitative reasoning skills to analyze and solve numerical problems
  4. Essential Skill 4

    Interpersonal Relations

    Interact effectively with people
  5. Essential Skill 5

    Adaptability and Leadership

    Adapt to changing circumstances while leading and supporting others
  6. Essential Skill 6

    Professionalism

    Perform professionally within their chosen field of study or occupation
  7. Essential Skill 7

    Civic Engagement

    Engage in civic life to improve society
  8. Essential Skill 8

    Collaboration and Teamwork

    Collaborate and work in teams
  9. Essential Skill 9

    Knowledge Application

    Apply academic knowledge, skills and abilities to their chosen career
  10. Essential Skill 10

    Information Literacy

    Use information for decision making
