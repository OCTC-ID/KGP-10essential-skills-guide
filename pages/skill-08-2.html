Essential Skill 8: Collaboration and Teamwork

Definition: Graduates will collaborate with colleagues, become effective team members, and manage conflict.

From Benchmark to Milestone

BenchmarkCollege entry

Responds promptly to messages and requests for information.

→

Milestone 8.2End of two years of college

Addresses conflicts constructively, communicating expectations and adjusting own behavior based on feedback received.

I Can Statements

  • I can address disagreements or tension within a team in a professional and constructive way.
  • I can communicate my expectations clearly without creating unnecessary conflict.
  • I can receive feedback from teammates and adjust my behavior accordingly.

What to Look For

A student working at the Milestone level:

  • Responds to team conflict or disagreement without dismissing, avoiding, or escalating the issue
  • Clearly states expectations or concerns using professional language
  • Demonstrates a change in approach or behavior following peer or instructor feedback
  • Gives feedback to teammates that is specific, respectful, and actionable

Assessment Ideas by Discipline

Technical & Trades

  • In-PersonDuring team projects or lab work, observe how students handle disagreements about process or procedure — note whether they address it directly and professionally or ignore/escalate it
  • Online / BlackboardUse a structured Peer Review where students give written feedback on a teammate's contribution; assess for specificity, professionalism, and actionability
  • Lab / HybridBuild a mid-project team check-in where students identify one area of friction and propose a constructive resolution — assessable as a brief written or verbal submission

Health Sciences

  • In-PersonDuring simulation or clinical role-play, assess whether students communicate concerns about care decisions professionally and constructively with teammates or supervisors
  • Online / BlackboardAssign a case scenario involving a team disagreement; students respond in a Discussion describing how they would address it constructively — assess for professionalism and specificity
  • Lab / HybridUse Peer Review following a team simulation; peers assess whether feedback given was respectful, specific, and focused on improvement

General Education & Business

  • In-PersonUse a structured group activity with a built-in conflict scenario; observe how students navigate it and assess using a professional communication rubric
  • Online / BlackboardUse Blackboard's Peer Review feature on a team deliverable; assess the quality of feedback given — not just whether it was submitted
  • HybridAssign a reflection Journal after a team activity where students describe a moment of friction, how they responded, and what they would adjust — directly assessable

Practical Tips for Faculty

  • Conflict in group work is an assessment opportunity, not just a management problem — building in structured reflection turns it into observable, gradable behavior
  • Peer feedback quality is assessable; give students a rubric for giving feedback, not just receiving it
  • A mid-project team check-in surfaces communication issues early enough to address them and gives you a natural observation point
  • In online courses, the written record of team communication in Discussions or Group spaces makes professional tone and responsiveness directly visible
