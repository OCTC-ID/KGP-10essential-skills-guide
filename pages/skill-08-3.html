Essential Skill 8: Collaboration and Teamwork

Definition: Graduates will collaborate with colleagues, become effective team members, and manage conflict.

From Benchmark to Milestone

BenchmarkCollege entry

Completes tasks on time and recognizes how individual responsibilities impact team goals.

→

Milestone 8.3End of two years of college

Identifies tasks required to reach a goal and learns about team members' assets to support the distribution of roles and responsibilities.

I Can Statements

  • I can identify what tasks need to be completed for a team to reach its goal.
  • I can recognize what my teammates do well and use that to help distribute work effectively.
  • I can take on a role that fits both the team's needs and my own strengths.

What to Look For

A student working at the Milestone level:

  • Participates in breaking a team goal into specific, assignable tasks
  • Can identify at least one strength or asset each teammate brings to the project
  • Accepts and fulfills a role that was assigned based on team need, not just personal preference
  • Completes their assigned responsibilities in a way that supports rather than delays the team

Assessment Ideas by Discipline

Technical & Trades

  • In-PersonDuring a multi-step team project, require students to create a task list with assigned roles before beginning — assess whether the distribution reflects team members' skills and project needs
  • Online / BlackboardUse a Group space where students submit a team planning document identifying tasks, roles, and the rationale for each assignment — directly assessable
  • Lab / HybridAt project completion, use Peer Review to assess whether each member fulfilled their role effectively and supported the team's progress

Health Sciences

  • In-PersonDuring a care team simulation, assign roles and assess whether students understand how their specific responsibilities connect to patient outcomes and team function
  • Online / BlackboardAssign a team case study where students divide responsibilities, document who is doing what and why, and submit individual contributions for review
  • Lab / HybridUse a structured debrief after a team simulation — each student identifies one teammate strength and explains how it contributed to the team's effectiveness

General Education & Business

  • In-PersonRequire teams to submit a roles and responsibilities document at the start of any group project — assess for clarity, task specificity, and evidence that strengths were considered
  • Online / BlackboardUse Blackboard Groups with assigned roles; each student submits their individual contribution separately so accountability is clear and assessable
  • HybridBuild a team reflection into the project where each member describes their role, what they contributed, and how the distribution of work supported the goal

Practical Tips for Faculty

  • Requiring a team planning document at the start of a group project makes roles visible and gives you an early assessment point before the project is finished
  • Individual accountability within group work is the key to making this assessable — design assignments so each member submits something that reflects their specific role
  • Peer assessment of role fulfillment is more informative than a general "teamwork" rating — ask peers to assess specific behaviors, not overall impressions
  • Helping students identify each other's strengths is a skill worth teaching explicitly — a brief structured activity at the start of a team project pays dividends throughout
